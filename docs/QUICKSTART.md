# Planwharf quick start

1. **Install** [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) and at least one AI coding tool you are signed in to: Codex, Claude Code, Grok CLI or Antigravity.
2. **Download** `Planwharf-vX.Y.Z.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/benitz94/Planwharf/releases/latest), extract it, and open `Planwharf.exe` (Windows) or run `sh ./planwharf.sh` (macOS/Linux).
3. **Team** (top right):
   - choose a lead;
   - press **Build** on one model;
   - press **Verify** on another, ideally from a different company.
4. **+ New project**: a name and a new folder.
5. Tell the lead what to build, in plain words, then press **Use as the goal** under your message.
6. **Let the crew do it**.

Follow the work in the chat. Press **Stop work** at any time; **Resume** continues later. When it finishes, check **Files** and try the result in the project folder. Nothing is committed or published automatically.

More: [first project guide](HOW-TO.md) · [the chat view](CHAT-VIEW.md) · [user guide](USER-GUIDE.md).
