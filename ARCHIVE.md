# Planwharf archive

This branch keeps the working material that used to live on `main`, unchanged:

- `.review/`: review runs and their evidence.
- `handoff/`: handoff notes between work sessions.
- `engineering-logs/`: CI output (the CI now keeps it as a workflow artifact).
- `validation/full-flow-20261103/`, `validation/*.png`, `validation/report.txt`: validation evidence.
- `docs/evidence/` and the dated reports in `docs/` (verifications, acceptances, phase reports, maintenance).

It is a snapshot of `main` at the moment the material was moved, so every file is where it was and every link inside it still works. Nothing here is built, tested or released. The app lives on `main`.
